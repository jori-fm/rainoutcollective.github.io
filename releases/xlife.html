X Life

jayys

Album • Released April 17, 2026 • RAIN-009

About The Project

X Life is the highly anticipated debut album by jayys under RAINOUT. Moving forward from his earlier abstract works and the disquiet era, this project cements his evolution as a versatile artist.

It features collaborative appearances from osecho and RAINOUT's own sollaceee!.
