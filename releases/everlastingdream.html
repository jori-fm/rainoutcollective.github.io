About The Project

everlasting dream is a chaotic but smooth ambient EP, led by jayys. The album pushes boundaries with distorted textures and high-energy tracks.

Featuring intense collaborative energy from the RAINOUT roster, including appearances by sollaceee! and Kaye Arcy on the track "garden", the project continues to establish the collective's aggressive, boundary-pushing sound.
