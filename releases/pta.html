Pushing The Agenda

blueZoe

Album • Released May 29, 2026 • RAIN-010

About The Project

Originally started in early February, Pushing The Agenda serves as blueZoe's highly anticipated debut album under RAINOUT.

With 12 tracks of runtime, the project aimed to be an entire album filled with positive, hopeful vibes—a deliberate deviation from the overwhelming negativity often found in the underground scene. The project went through many different versions, collaborations, and hectic changes until picking up pace when Jori sent Zoe a motivational message, helping her polish and finish the album into what it is today.

Totaling 12 features, the tracklist contains appearances from many of her RAINOUT peers as well as V0CALYST, Claire Eterna, jayys, Castle Belmont, and sollaceee!. It also includes a solo complextro-style song as the outro, showcasing her versatile production roots.
