About The Project

ZOMBIEBOY (stylized as "ZOMᗺIƎᗺOY") is V0CALYST's debut album under RAINOUT. The nine-track project was primarily produced by lunamaryllis and shinrei, creating a blend of hardcore rage, experimental industrial hip-hop, and digicore.

Production began in July 2025. Following the arrival of lunamaryllis, shinrei, and krewlty to the collective, the four began collaborating on RAINOUT's first major project. The release of "believe the kids" on August 1st marked V0CALYST's first major track, featuring production by lunamaryllis and engineering by shinrei. Two weeks later, "ggysm" was released, marking another production by lunamaryllis (credited as lunamarylliskuron). The album has two features, both from hellayercs. "SOULSNATCHER" was the final track completed for the album; appearing as the eighth track, it is currently being developed into RAINOUT's first official music video.
