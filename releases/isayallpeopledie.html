About The Project

“The knife laid buried in the southern sand, the silence split by the rain that ran cold down my skin. I collapsed to my knees, the sky breaking open with me, pleading with God that this was nothing but a sick dream. It wasn’t. The blood of my only lover mingled with the rain, her body laying lifeless on the shore. Only then did the veil lift, and I awoke from the blindness I had been cursed under my whole life. I looked at her one last time, at what remained, and then I turned away, back toward the waiting sea. From the sand I drew the knife again and stepped back, ashen with what I had done, and pressed it to my throat, as if the ocean might take the rest of me too.”

I say all people die searching for a place to belong is Avalon’s debut album under RAINOUT. It stands as the second installment in the “%” Trilogy, of which was kickstarted just over 14 months prior to its release. The album was executively produced by Avalon herself, with additional production and direction from MARLOW on tracks such as “blood in the rain”, “blush”, and “hazy”.

The beginning of this project stretches back to winter of 2024, marked by countless months devoted to writing personal lore, ideas, and crafting demo tracks that over time carved the album’s final vision. It wasn’t until the summer of 2025, however, that Avalon began recording full length songs for the project, with tracks such as “candle light” and “threw it away” tracing back to June 2025, a full nine months before the album’s release.

This project expresses Avalon’s personal struggles with love, mental health, and her feelings in general that could not be expressed in any other form. She put her heart, soul and blood into this. This project was made by her, for her.
