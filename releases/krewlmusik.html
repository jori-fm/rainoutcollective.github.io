About The Project

#krewlmusik is the debut album from krewlty under RAINOUT. A high-energy collision of digicore, trap, and experimental electronic sounds, the project showcases krewlty's versatility as both a vocalist and producer.

The album is mostly solo and features some collaboration within the collective, with features from V0CALYST, hellayercs, and lunamaryllis alongside krewlty's own production. It marks a significant milestone in the RAINOUT sound, blending aggressive basslines with melodic hooks.
