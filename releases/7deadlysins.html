7 deadly sins

V0CALYST

EP • Released Apr 3, 2026 • 13 Min

About The Project

7 deadly sins is an exploration of its namesake, serving as a standout EP from V0CALYST. The project dives deep into the corners of the human condition, rendered through digital textures, contorted bass, and raw vocal performances.

The EP boasts an incredible lineup of intricate, atmospheric production from eventgardian, hellayercs, lunamaryllis, cindy yearns, and foyu. Blending experimental underground sounds with heavy collaborative energy from across the RAINOUT roster, it is a challenging and unapologetic release.
