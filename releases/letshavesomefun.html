About The Project

let's have some fun is the debut RAINOUT EP from lunamaryllis. A chaotic yet vibrant exploration of the underground, the project fuses gritty digicore textures with high-octane hyperpop and pounding EDM rhythms.

Serving as a playground for lunamaryllis's experimental sound design, the EP features collaborative energy from Dani Kiyoko and RAINOUT's own V0CALYST. The project also boasts key production credits from lexycat and hywel, delivering a polished yet raw sonic experience that defines the next wave of the collective.
