avalon

On the % Trilogy, Her Identity, and the Future

jori
Tell me what's on your mind.
avalon
A lot of different, unexplainable feelings. There are so many different thoughts going through my head right now, but they all come back down to one thing, which is working on my craft.
jori
I can tell you're very focused on working on your craft, especially with the way you put together your grail project that was put out, "i say all people die searching for a place to belong"—aka the second installment of the % trilogy. What made you conjure up this project?
Cover for "i say all people die searching for a place to belong" / Made by Loctify
avalon
I used to write a lot of short stories and personal lore to help me come up with ideas and concepts for the original % project, and in doing so, I came up with the album title which has been around for the better part of a year now. The title was the last words of a man who had taken the life of his wife unknowingly on a beach, and later on took his own. The last words of that man were, “All people die searching for a place to belong.” That story in particular means a lot to me, and I wish to push it into the light as time progresses.
jori
What is the spectrum that is the % trilogy? How well do you think the projects go together? Since you have confirmed yourself you're working on a third installment already, which is something a lot of people will look forward to.
avalon
I feel that as of right now, the story behind Phase 1 and 2 is very obscure as I’ve not made it public in full, but when Phase 3 comes around it will all begin to come together and all the pieces will just click. And on that topic, Phase 3 is something I aim to be my greatest work yet. In all fields. I am already spending a large portion of my time working on concepts and ideas. I think that once this project is released to the world, everybody—even I—will be shocked at the leap. If you listen to any of my projects and don’t feel anything in conclusion, then I have failed.
"If you listen to any of my projects and don’t feel anything in conclusion, then I have failed."
jori
That is a heavy standard to hold yourself to, but it completely shows in the work. Speaking of those obscure pieces in Phase 1 and 2... do you enjoy keeping the listeners in the dark and watching them theorize, or is it agonizing having to hold back those secrets until Phase 3 is finally ready?
Avalon's main image from her Spotify Artist gallery (3.6.2026)
avalon
A bit of both. I actually know somebody who had been trying to figure out what each song on % meant, and I find it quite amusing. However, all I want is for people to understand the true meaning behind these projects because at the end of the day, % was created for me. Once the glass is spilt it will all make sense, but for now I must keep quiet and continue crafting my greatest piece yet. They know there is a story, but are unable to flip the pages.
jori
It's great to know how much these projects mean to you; people will know that you are actually authentic when it comes to your work. What are your plans when the % trilogy is finished? Are you going to dive into a different world of sound? Will you work more with people from RAINOUT? How should you continue your journey?
avalon
I am honestly not sure as to what I will do after that point. I only have 3 projects planned, all of which I aspire to release before my eighteenth birthday, which include the Gilded Ache Deluxe, % Phase 3, and my self-titled album. I will definitely be working with more members from RAINOUT though, as there is a lot I want to create with them. I have a second collaborative tape with Loctify which will be releasing very, very soon, and I am planning on releasing an EP with shinrei later this year. Of course, these are only the main ones that are already planned—I want to work with everyone. Loctify, V0CALYST, luna, shinrei, suuni, sollaceee!, danny, jacob... literally everyone. I love RAINOUT and everyone in it, and I am incredibly grateful to be a part of it.
Cover for "GILDED ACHE"
jori
I am very much looking forward to how you'll blend with these artists! Your versatility is very unique and you make an amazing addition to this collective. And I also cannot wait for that Gilded Ache deluxe!! I want to dive into another topic, being your transition from your old alias. What moved you to become avalon?
avalon
A mix of a few things. Firstly was of course my transition from male to female in which my deadname was literally in my old alias, but also the fact that the name and entire discography from that era was incredibly sloppy and soulless, and I was incredibly embarrassed for that to be who I was. Now, the name avalon came from a song I discovered in 2023 with the same title by an artist named Zeruel. The song got taken down and reuploaded as a worse version for some reason, but I thankfully still have the original demo downloaded. The song saved my life in a very difficult time, and even to this day I still listen to it. Honestly, it inspired a lot of my newer work too. I'm not who I once was. avalon and whoever that was prior are two completely different people.
Cover for avalon's birthday EP '^_^'
jori
I really appreciate that you shared that. I think it takes a lot of strength to close the door on an old identity. How different does the actual creative process feel? Does making music now feel like breathing fresh air, or did you have to relearn how to express yourself?
avalon
It's sort of a strange feeling. Yes, whenever I release a song or create a project or do anything as avalon it feels great, and I am grateful that I'm free to be able to express who I truly am, but there has always been this background feeling that misses a part of the old me. There is just something so captivating about my old production and creative mindset that I cannot seem to replicate. It feels like the things that made me who I was are slipping out of my grasp. But that's okay, I think it's life's way of telling me to not become stuck in the past, and to create more that revolves around who I am now, not someone who I used to be.
"I think it's life's way of telling me to not become stuck in the past, and to create more that revolves around who I am now, not someone who I used to be."
jori
That's a great way to look at it! Growth means having to let go of a few things, but it makes room for exactly who you are meant to be now. To wrap it up, for everyone who's waiting for that Gilded Ache deluxe and % Phase 3, what is one thing you want them to know before they hear what you've been working on?
avalon
Keep your expectations high. I crave the pressure. I promise that it is worth the wait, and maybe if they are patient enough, I will reveal the truth behind % sooner than you think.
