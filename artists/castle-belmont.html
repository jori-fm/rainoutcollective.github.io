Biography

Castle Belmont is a musician from Italy. He started producing at the age of 12, developing his sound early on.

In 2025, he rebranded from his former producer name "yngcloud" and began experimenting with his own voice, recording and releasing original tracks. His music first appeared on SoundCloud before expanding to all major streaming platforms.
