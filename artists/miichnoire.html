Biography

miichnoire is a passionate visual artist based in Riverside, California. Fascinated by creativity from a young age, she found solace in the act of creating art herself.

She is currently dedicated to studying and refining her craft, determined to find the "realest version" of herself and embrace it. While primarily focused on visuals, she also plans to expand into music production in the near future.
