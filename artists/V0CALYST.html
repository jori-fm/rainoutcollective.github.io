Biography

VOCALYST (Kevin) is a rapper and vocalist from Florida. He started making music on SoundCloud using type beats from YouTube.

In January 2025, he joined RAINOUT and began releasing music on streaming platforms, both solo and with members like lunamaryllis and shinrei.
