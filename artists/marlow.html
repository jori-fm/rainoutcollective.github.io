Biography

Ellie (professionally known as MARLOW) is a multi-genre producer, guitarist, bassist, and sound designer from Arizona. She began producing on GarageBand in 2019, which eventually led to her transitioning to FL Studio in 2021.

Later that year, she launched a YouTube channel that now boasts over 7,000 subscribers and 11 million total views. She is well known for her frequent collaborations with artists like avalon, Loctify, ezcodylee, and many more.

MARLOW's production spans a massive sonic spectrum—ranging from melodic rage beats inspired by Ginseng and Prettifun, to emotional heavy shoegaze influenced by Whirr, all the way to gritty industrial metal inspired by Mick Gordon and Nine Inch Nails.
