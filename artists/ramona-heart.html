Biography

Ramona Heart is an artist and producer hailing from Houston, Texas. Her musical journey began in middle school when she was introduced to the guitar, which eventually led her to transition into music production using FL Studio in 2019.

Drawing heavy inspiration from shoegaze, ambient, and pop music, she continuously blends these influences together to form her own distinct, atmospheric sound.
