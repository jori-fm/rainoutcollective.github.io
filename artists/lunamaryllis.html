Biography

Lunamaryllis (aka 'Luna Amaryllis' & formerly 'sai') is a musician whose first release, Luna, debuted on SoundCloud in July 2024. She is known for her connection to lexycat, and her release with SVPACYBERIA 'a letter 2 my past'

Known for her electronic, hiphop/rap, dnb, & EDM sound, lunamaryllis debuted her first physical release with SVPACYBERIA, being "a letter 2 my past" as well as her debut EP with RAINOUT, "let's have some fun".
