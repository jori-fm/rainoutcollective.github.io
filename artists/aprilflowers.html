Aprilflowers

Member since September 8, 2026

Biography

Aprilflowers is a New York-based visual artist and musician whose work serves as a portal for dream-like escapism. A lifelong illustrator, she is known for blending anime-inspired digital art with intricate photobashing and heavy textures, typically framing her pieces within a signature square canvas.

Driven by nostalgia and raw emotion, she draws her core creative inspiration from the music of her friends. After breaking into the scene through striking cover art and her own audio production, her collaborative work with Ramona Heart caught the attention of the collective. Aprilflowers officially joined RAINOUT on September 8, 2026, alongside 0gen, cementing her place in the group's visual identity.
