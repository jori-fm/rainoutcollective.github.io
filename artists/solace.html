Biography

Solace (aka 'sollaceee!') is a rapper, singer, and producer hailing from Inglewood, CA. He began his artistic journey by crafting instrumental tapes and EPs, eventually synthesizing the raw energy of rage, punk, and digicore with modern pop sensibilities.

In 2025, he released his full-length album, "KIDS CAN'T KILL!". The project marks a definitive evolution for the artist—stepping out from behind the boards to command the microphone, handling both production and vocals with renewed intensity.
