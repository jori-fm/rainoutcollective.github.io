Biography

melles (Melody Tucker) is a rapper, vocalist and producer based in New York. She has made music since 2022, but started releasing music in 2024.

She joined RAINOUT in January 2026, making energetic electronic infused trap and rage music. She also makes ambient and experimental music under her “biotechnic” project. Despite being signed to SAKRAMENTO primarily, she still remains apart of the collective similar to andiekyte, cigstench, and sadcrib.
