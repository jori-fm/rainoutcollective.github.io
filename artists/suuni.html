Biography

As one of the four founding pioneers of RAINOUT, Maryland-based artist suuni is a rapidly evolving creative force. Moving swiftly from initial BandLab concepts to full FL Studio production in just a few months, suuni refuses to be boxed in.

Blending intricate sample chopping, dynamic production, and striking visual art, they are quietly crafting their next era. Tune in to see what the future holds.
