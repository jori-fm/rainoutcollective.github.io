Biography

Sorrysuumi (also known as 'imsorrybutive') is a producer, DJ, and artist from New York. She began making music as a hobby in 2019 before pursuing it professionally in 2024.

Drawing heavy inspiration from 2010s dubstep and EDM, her sound blends nostalgic electronic influences with a modern hyperpop feel.
