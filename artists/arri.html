Biography

arri is a singer/producer from Malaysia. Drawing inspiration from artists such as Lucy Bedroque, h3artch3rades, Twikipedia, and Bunii, he blends dreamy textures with intimate vocals and his signature “Asian fuckboy” timbre.

He started out creating covers and is now diving into original music across multiple genres—including acoustic, indie rock and pop, aiming to craft a unique sonic identity that’s both impactful and personal.
