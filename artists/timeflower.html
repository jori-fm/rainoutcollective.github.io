Biography

Timeflower (a.k.a. rainconsolation) is a 15 year old multi-genre pianist, producer and rapper from Mexico residing in the US. Their style is fluid, yet remains deeply personal.

They draw inspiration from various genres like IDM, ambient, free jazz, sound collage and underground rap, as well as aesthetic inspiration from modern / postmodern literature and abstract art.
