Biography

PEZZZ! is a versatile experimental artist and producer known for his extensive collaborative work across the underground scene. In 2025, he teamed up with vinnycent and Coalescence to release the sprawling, 14-track collaborative album POLYVINYLCHLORIDE.

As a heavy-hitting producer within RAINOUT, PEZZZ! has lent his art to some of the group's most notable drops. His production credits include tracks on sollaceee!'s KIDS CAN'T KILL and appleLove, as well as V0CALYST's standout single "Pack a Punch".

Constantly expanding his creative toolkit, PEZZZ! is currently gearing up to step out from behind the boards and incorporate his own vocals into his upcoming solo releases.
