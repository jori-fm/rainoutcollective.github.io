V0CALYST announces "SuperBlue"

By Jori • 9/23/2026

V0CALYST has officially announced SuperBlue. Serving as his second EP this year following 7 deadly sins and executively produced by SAKRAMENTO affiliate andiekyte, the project is locked in for release this Saturday.

JORI: how did you feel while working on superblue? What obstacles did you face and what was your favorite moments
V0CALYST: working on superblue wasn’t a plan i had at first in terms of making it an ep with the name it did but i had fun involving sakramento into a project like this

i honestly didn’t face a lot of struggles while making this project besides the general direction of how the music would sound and the themes of the songs and project it’s self

i enjoyed making the songs as much as i would with my other projects so that definitely had to be my favorite part

The Vision

The visual direction matches the collaborative energy of the project. The official cover art for SuperBlue was fully designed by andiekyte, tying the aesthetic and sonic themes together perfectly.
