About Us

RAINOUT is an independent collective, label, and a creative group focused on embracing underground music diversity and appreciation. We value experimentation, collaboration, and giving artists space to build their sound.

This site hosts our latest releases and artist profiles. Stick around — more is coming.

What We Do

  • Curate and release digital singles, EPs, and LPs.
  • Offer light-weight support on artwork, rollout, and distribution.
  • Connect artists for features, remixes, and cross-project ideas.

FAQ

Is this a label or a collective?

Both. We operate as both a creative collective and an independent record label. We work together, share resources, and collaborate extensively on projects. As a label, we handle the business of our art.

How do I join or collaborate?

You can reach out to us in our Discord community server or you can contact us at rainout.co@aol.com

Do you sell physical releases?

Yes! Head to rainout.store to see what is available ^_^

Can I use your visuals?

Please consult our Creative Director, Shinrei, or the owner, Jori, through our email or the Discord community server.
